Fake Review Detection

A comparative study of statistical, sequential and transformer models for detecting AI-generated product reviews.

May – Jun 2026

Code for Fake Review Detection (opens in a new tab)

Problem

AI-generated fake reviews are cheap to produce and hard for people to spot. We wanted to know how much model sophistication actually buys you.

Approach

  • Baseline: TF-IDF features with Logistic Regression.
  • PosAtt-BiLSTM: GloVe embeddings, positional encoding and Bahdanau attention.
  • Fine-tuned DistilBERT, plus zero-shot Phi-4-mini as an LLM reference point.
  • Served the best model through a Flask web app.

My role

Overall planning and pipeline. Trained the Logistic Regression and DistilBERT models, and took part in the results analysis.
