Voice Chess

Play chess against Stockfish by voice, with a fine-tuned Whisper and a wake-word model that run entirely in the browser.

Sep – Oct 2026

Live demo for Voice Chess (opens in a new tab)Code for Voice Chess (opens in a new tab)Model for Voice Chess (opens in a new tab)

Problem

Speech recognisers that work out of the box handle chess notation badly ("rook to e4", "knight d2"), and browser speech APIs send your audio to a server. I wanted hands-free, accessible chess that runs on-device.

Approach

  • Fine-tuned Whisper-base on 40,000 synthetic Piper TTS clips plus slices of LibriSpeech, Speech Commands and AN4, then exported it to 8-bit ONNX so it runs in the browser.
  • Trained a 21k-parameter wake-word CNN from scratch.
  • Every move is read back and only played after you confirm, and you can correct it ("no, knight d2"). A fuzzy parser combines up to 5 recogniser guesses.
  • Error analysis led to targeted training data, for example rank 3 being heard as "tree".

My role

Everything: data generation, model training, evaluation and the web app.
