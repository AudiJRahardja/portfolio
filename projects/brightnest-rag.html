Brightnest Knowledge Assistants

One retrieval engine behind two chatbots, for employees and for customers, that answers in English or Indonesian with citations and never leaks internal data.

Nov 2026

Code for Brightnest Knowledge Assistants (opens in a new tab)

Problem

Company chatbots have to give grounded answers from internal documents, and must never show HR-only or internal information to someone who isn't allowed to see it.

Approach

  • Hybrid retrieval: vectors plus BM25 with rank fusion, then a cross-encoder reranker. Follow-up questions are rewritten, two-part questions are split, and outdated policies are filtered out.
  • Separate public, internal and restricted collections with JWT roles (employee, manager, HR). Inputs are checked for prompt injection, and outputs are checked for leaks using hashed fingerprints.
  • LLM tools for leave balance, tickets, order status and store hours. An embeddable chat widget, plus an analytics page for feedback.
  • Eval harness of 86 internal, 82 external and 38 leak-attack questions, with an ablation table.

My role

Everything: retrieval design, security model, tools, evaluation and deployment config.
