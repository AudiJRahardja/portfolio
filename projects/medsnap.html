MedSnap

Point your phone at a medicine box: on-device OCR finds the active ingredients and dosages, even on blurry, tilted or sideways labels.

Apr – Oct 2026

Live demo for MedSnap (opens in a new tab)Code for MedSnap (opens in a new tab)

Problem

Medicine labels are small, glossy and often photographed at an angle. Generic OCR misses ingredients or reads the wrong dose, and you shouldn't have to upload a photo of your medication to a server.

Approach

  • Burst capture that keeps the sharpest frame, scored by Laplacian variance.
  • Channel selection, deskew, adaptive thresholding and multi-pass OCR across rotations, all written by hand in plain JavaScript.
  • OCR-aware fuzzy matching against about 340 drug names, then pairing each ingredient with its dose.
  • Reproducible benchmark of generated labels (easy, hard, colour and sideways sets) run headless in Puppeteer.

My role

Rebuilt the hackathon prototype on my own: the image pipeline, matching, benchmark and UI.
