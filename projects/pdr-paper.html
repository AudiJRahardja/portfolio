Performance Decay Rate (PDR): IEEE paper

How badly do ML models break when tabular data gets noisy? A new metric that separates a model's structural fragility from its baseline accuracy.

May – Sep 2026

Paper (IEEE Xplore) for Performance Decay Rate (PDR): IEEE paper (opens in a new tab)Code for Performance Decay Rate (PDR): IEEE paper (opens in a new tab)

Problem

Models are usually evaluated only on clean data. Real-world inputs come from imperfect sensors and messy databases, so we rarely know how fragile a model really is.

Approach

  • Added increasing additive white Gaussian noise (AWGN) to the numeric features of the UCI Heart Disease dataset.
  • Compared MLP, Random Forest, XGBoost and SVM in two settings: noisy data only at test time, and noisy data in both training and testing.
  • Measured F1, MCC and AUC-ROC, plus the proposed Performance Decay Rate (PDR), which isolates how much a model degrades independently of how good it was to begin with.

My role

First author: main researcher and lead writer. Designed the method and degradation framework, implemented all four models, ran the evaluations, and wrote the analysis, conclusion, slides and video.
